Description
Maze Obby is a giant maze adventure floating in the sky. Run through 12 themed mazes in a row, each one bigger and trickier than the last: golden sand walls over clear blue water, leafy hedge gardens, yellow brick mazes, rainbow blocks, bright grass labyrinths, dark hedges on wooden boards, classic Roblox studs, the yellow backrooms, slippery frozen ice caves, a glowing lava castle, candy cane walls and a neon galaxy under the stars. Follow the beam of light to every exit, collect the Win gems hidden in dead ends and reach every checkpoint to earn Wins, then escape the last maze to win big - and the rewards restart for another run! Become a Free Morph (a Noob, a Banana, a Robot, a Knight, a Ninja, a Dino and more), open the treasure chest and the lucky block, and spend your Wins on the Speed Coil, Gravity Coil, Super Coil, the Magic Carpet to fly over the walls, the Ray Gun and the Deadly Sword, Fling All, Kill All, Skip Stage, Skip to End and colourful trails. No in-app purchases: everything is earned with Wins. Race your friends to the finish!
